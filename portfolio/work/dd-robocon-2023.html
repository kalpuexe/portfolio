DD-Robocon 2023

Two omnidirectional robots, one multidisciplinary team, four days in Delhi.

Role
Team lead
Timeline
June 2023
Event
Thyagaraj Sports Complex, New Delhi · 15–18 June 2023
Organisers
IIT Delhi with Prasar Bharati

The brief

DD-Robocon is the Indian leg of the ABU Asia-Pacific Robot Contest. The 2023 season required two cooperating robots — nicknamed “Rabbit” and “Elephant” — each with different movement and manipulation demands, built to a fixed rules envelope and a hard competition date.

How I approached it

The hard constraint was not the engineering, it was the calendar. Two robots had to be designed, fabricated, wired and debugged in parallel by a team drawn from several disciplines, most of whom had not built a competition robot before.

I split the work along subsystem lines with clear interfaces between them, so mechanical, electronics and controls could progress without blocking each other, and kept the drive bases as similar as the rules allowed to avoid solving the same problem twice.

What we built

Two omnidirectional robots on aluminium extrusion frames:

  • Mecanum and omniwheel drive systems for holonomic movement
  • Custom fabricated chassis and manipulation mechanisms per robot
  • On-board electronics and control wiring, debugged in the pit
  • A shared design language between both machines to reuse parts and knowledge

Where it landed

Both robots were completed and competed at Thyagaraj Sports Complex in New Delhi from 15 to 18 June 2023, in the contest organised by IIT Delhi in collaboration with Prasar Bharati.

The lasting value was managerial rather than mechanical: it was the first time I owned scope, sequencing and a team against a date that would not move.
