RECRUIT-AI

An agentic AI assistant that screens resumes end to end.

Role
Product Manager
Timeline
2024–25
Context
PM capstone, Masai School with Vishlesan i-Hub, IIT Patna
Status
Working prototype

The problem

I started by mapping how recruiters at small and mid-size tech companies actually spend their week, rather than assuming where the pain was. The bottleneck turned out to sit right at the front of the funnel: manual resume screening was consuming roughly half of a recruiter's time.

That matters more than it first looks. Screening is upstream of every other hiring activity, so any delay there compounds through the whole pipeline — and it is the least differentiated work a recruiter does.

How I approached it

I ran structured user research with recruiters, defined the SME-recruiter persona, and framed the opportunity with Jobs-to-Be-Done: the recruiter is not hiring a resume parser, they are hiring a way to get to a credible shortlist before the hiring manager loses patience.

From there I wrote the PRD and prioritised the feature set down to what would actually prove the thesis.

What I built

Rather than stopping at documentation, I built the working backend as an agentic workflow in n8n:

  • Resume ingestion and parsing from incoming applications
  • LLM-based scoring of each candidate against the role's requirements
  • Automated decision emails, so the loop closes without a human step
  • A Figma prototype for the recruiter-facing review surface

Where it landed

The prototype was validated against a target of 60% less time spent screening and a 3× faster time-to-hire for SME tech companies. The interesting result was less the automation itself and more that recruiters were willing to trust a shortlist they did not assemble by hand — provided the reasoning was visible to them.
