About the project

The Flying Deck was exhibited under the Mobility theme at DIPEX 2024, the state-level working-model exhibition run at Terna Engineering College in Navi Mumbai from 7 to 10 March 2024.

DIPEX requires a functioning model rather than a poster or a simulation, so entries have to survive four days of continuous demonstration in front of judges and visitors — a useful constraint, and a different discipline from building something that only has to work once.

Where it landed

Selected and exhibited at the state-level event, with participation certified by the organisers.
