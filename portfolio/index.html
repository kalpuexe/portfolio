Co-inventor & design lead · 2023–25

Automated Guided Vehicle for Crop Fertilization

A granted design patent, and a prototype that actually drives.

The problem

Broadcast fertiliser spreading over-applies nutrients across a field — expensive for the farmer and hard on the soil.

What I did

Led the mechanical design and used MoSCoW prioritisation to cut an over-scoped concept down to a buildable MVP: hopper, metering mechanism, servo-actuated spreader arms and Arduino control on a wheeled chassis.

Where it landed

Design Patent No. 436182-001, registered 30 October 2024 and issued 16 December 2024 by the Patent Office, Government of India. I kept developing it afterwards — a second generation moves to four-wheel sprung suspension with a cargo bed and twin arms.

Independent research · i2i Stage 2, Eaton India Foundation + COEP · 2023 – present

Continuum Soft Robot Arm

An arm that bends along its whole length instead of pivoting at joints.

The idea

A rigid robot arm is a chain of links turning at discrete joints, which works inside a cell built around it and badly almost everywhere else. A continuum arm bends continuously, so there are no joints to collide with.

What I did

Built the mechanism before modelling it — a stack of laser-cut discs on a flexible backbone with tendons through the edges. Pulling a tendon shortens one side and the whole structure curves.

Where it landed

Selected for Stage 2 innovation funding under Ignited Innovators of India, run by COEP Technological University with the Eaton India Foundation. Still open research.

Product Manager · Masai School × IIT Patna · 2024–25

RECRUIT-AI

An agentic AI assistant that screens resumes end to end.

The problem

I mapped recruiter workflows at small and mid-size tech companies and found manual screening was eating roughly half of a recruiter's week — the bottleneck sitting in front of every hiring decision.

What I did

Ran structured user research, defined the SME-recruiter persona, framed the work with Jobs-to-Be-Done, and wrote the PRD. Then built the thing: an n8n agentic backend that parses resumes, scores them against the role with an LLM, and sends decision emails without a human in the loop.

Where it landed

A working prototype validated against a target of 60% less screening time and 3× faster time-to-hire.

Product & build · Personal project · 2025

SaveSmart

A budgeting app that rebuilds your plan after an emergency.

The problem

Budgeting apps are good at telling you that you overspent. None of them do anything about it — so a single ₹5,000 emergency quietly takes the savings goal down with it.

What I did

Tore down YNAB, Mint, Monarch, Walnut and INDmoney to find the gap, then positioned SaveSmart on goal protection rather than expense tracking. Designed and built the React app around a reprioritisation engine that automatically reallocates discretionary spending once an emergency hits.

Where it landed

A working Android APK aimed at India's outcome-based budgeting market.
